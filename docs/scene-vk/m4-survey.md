## Order of work

1. **Sound objects** (9 scenes, 8 of them refused for nothing else): decode the mp3 (a vendored single-header decoder), loop it through a PipeWire playback stream at the object's `volume` times the bridge's volume, silent when paused or muted. The spectrum uniforms (20 scenes use `g_AudioSpectrum16*`, one the 32 and 64 band ones) come from capturing the default sink monitor, only when a shader or script reads them.
2. **Bloom** (7 scenes, two with the HDR settings): a threshold, blur and add chain on the scene target.
3. **Text and SceneScript** (5 clocks, plus scripts on `rate` and `visible`): FreeType layout of `text` objects (4 fonts, one a system font: fontconfig), and QuickJS-ng running `update(value)` with `createScriptProperties`, `Date`, `Vec2/Vec3`, `thisLayer.*`, `engine.frametime/canvasSize/registerAudioBuffers`, `input.cursorWorldPosition`. `thisScene.createLayer/getLayerIndex/sortLayer` (two scenes) last.
4. **`_rt_FullFrameBuffer` base textures** (3 scenes): a layer whose material samples the scene so far.
5. **Camera shake** (one scene: amplitude 0.5, roughness 1, speed 3).
